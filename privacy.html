Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

YT Shorts is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who signs in with their own Google account.

What data it uses

With the owner's permission it uses Google APIs to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, read the status of those videos, and save copies to the owner's Google Drive. It does not access anyone else's data.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and files are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's Google account. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Google API Services

YT Shorts' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
